# Pivot — Product Positioning Document

## 1. Product in One Sentence

Pivot is an AI-powered athlete wellness platform that helps NCAA rowing coaches detect early signs of physical and mental strain before they cost a seat in the boat.

## 2. Target Market (MVP)

**Primary:** NCAA Division I & II men's and women's rowing programs in the United States.

- Decision-maker: Head coach, assistant coach, or Director of Rowing.
- End-user: Collegiate rowers (18–22 years old) and their coaching staff.
- Geography: United States (Ivy League, Big Ten, Pac-12, ACC, Big 12).

**Explicitly out of scope for MVP:**
- High school / junior rowing (under 18)
- Masters / club rowing
- International federations outside the U.S.
- Other sports (basketball is a future expansion track, not MVP)

## 3. Problem We Solve

Rowing is a high-volume, high-monotony sport. By the time an athlete tells a coach they are struggling, the damage is often already done:

- Overtraining → HRV decline + RHR elevation → poor boat moving → injury
- Sleep debt → emotional vulnerability → conflict within the crew → burnout
- Mental health symptoms → 44% of student-athletes report them, but few disclose early

Pivot surfaces the **first invisible signals** (HRV, sleep, subjective check-ins) and gives coaches a structured way to intervene before performance drops.

## 4. Core Value Proposition

For coaches: "Know who needs you today — without guessing."

For athletes: "Your coach sees you before you have to raise your hand."

## 5. Key Features (MVP)

1. **Apple Watch / Apple Health integration** — reads HRV, RHR, sleep, workouts.
2. **Daily 20-second check-in** — mood, motivation, fatigue, optional journal.
3. **AI Coach Insight** — personalized psychological-style guidance generated from 7-day data + today's check-in.
4. **Alert engine** — yellow / red / black warnings based on biometric + subjective trends.
5. **Intervention workflow** — coach views AI-generated suggestions, sends a nudge, athlete responds via check-in, alert closes with timestamp.
6. **Crew dashboard** — roster, aggregate trends, alert queue, athlete detail view.

## 6. Metrics That Matter

| Metric | Why it matters for rowing |
|--------|---------------------------|
| HRV (ms) | Recovery status; early overtraining signal |
| Resting HR (bpm) | Autonomic stress; rising = not recovering |
| Sleep hours | Biggest controllable recovery lever |
| Mood (1–5) | Mental health leading indicator |
| Motivation (1–10) | Burnout / engagement signal |
| Fatigue (1–10) | Training load tolerance |

## 7. Alert Logic

- **Yellow:** HRV declining 3 days + RHR rising; OR sleep < 6 hrs for 3 days.
- **Red:** HRV critically low + poor sleep + declining training output.
- **Black:** Multiple biometric warnings + mood ≤ 2 for 2 consecutive days.

## 8. Positioning vs. General Wellness Apps

| Pivot | Whoop / Garmin / Apple Fitness |
|-------|--------------------------------|
| Team-facing; coach gets crew overview | Individual-only |
| Combines biometrics + subjective check-ins | Mostly objective |
| Psychology-informed color and language | Performance / training focused |
| Intervention workflow closes the loop | No coach action loop |

## 9. Go-to-Market (8-Week Plan)

- **Week 1–2:** Finish MVP with 1–2 real athletes' historical Health Export data.
- **Week 3–4:** Pilot with Penn lightweight/heavyweight rowing (3–5 athletes, 1 coach).
- **Week 5–6:** Iterate on alert accuracy and coach UX based on feedback.
- **Week 7–8:** Prepare demo deck + live dashboard for Penn coaching staff.

## 10. Long-Term Vision

Expand to NCAA basketball, soccer, swimming, and track — but only after rowing PMF is proven.
